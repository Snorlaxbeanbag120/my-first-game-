Block Runner is a simple 3D game where you control a red cube moving through a minimalist  world of floating platforms. The goal is to keep moving forward without falling off – testing your timing  and precision.

**Features**

Minimalist 3D design
Smooth movement and jumping mechanics
Simple platform – based gameplay
Built using unity

**How to Play **

Move Forward – W 
Move Left/Right – A/D
Jump : Spacebar
Avoid falling of the platforms – once you fall, it’s game over!
